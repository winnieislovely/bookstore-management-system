## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional requirements of the **Bookstore Management System** (BMS): one integrated platform that runs the day-to-day operations of a single bookstore that sells books, stationery and other goods, hosts events, and operates a lending library space. It is the basis for design, construction and acceptance testing.

### 1.2 Scope

The BMS covers every operating activity of the store and its sales channels:

- **In-store retail** – point of sale, shifts and cash handling, returns, e-invoices.
- **Catalogue, inventory and purchasing** – books, stationery and other goods; quick stock-in; stock counts; suppliers; consignment stock.
- **Website back end (public API)** – the system holds and controls everything the website shows and does: products, prices, stock, collections, content pages, news, menu, carts, orders and customer accounts, exposed through a documented public API. Staff control **what appears** (content, collections, home-page sections, menu, stock display). The **website interface itself is built by a separate party and is not part of this system.**
- **Social media channels** – orders arriving through Facebook, Zalo, Instagram, TikTok and similar are entered by staff, tagged with their source, and reported by channel.
- **Online orders and delivery** – order inbox, fulfilment, GHN integration, cash on delivery, pre-orders and special orders.
- **Events** – free and paid events, registrations, check-in, merchandise, recurring programmes and event results.
- **Library** – separate lending collection, members, loans, deposits, late fees and lost-book charges.
- **Customers and loyalty** – customer profiles, points and member tiers, promotions.
- **Staff** – employee files, shift scheduling, fingerprint timekeeping and hourly payroll calculation.
- **Reporting and administration** – dashboards, sales, inventory, tax and audit reports; roles and permissions; settings; backup.

**Out of scope:** online payment gateways (VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay); the website front end (interface design and build); automatic order import from marketplaces; carriers other than GHN; paid membership programmes, seat or room rental, used-book buy-back and gift cards; marketing-campaign tooling; SMS and Zalo messages; customer emails other than reminders, account messages and e-invoice delivery; returns to suppliers other than consignment returns; general-ledger accounting beyond supplier payables and tax reports; statutory payroll (social insurance and personal income tax). Website exposure of events and the library is delivered in release R6 (Section 9).

### 1.4 Regulatory basis

- E-invoices follow the Vietnamese e-invoice regulation (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and its amendments). The store is a company declaring VAT by the deduction method.
- Personal data handling follows Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. Fingerprints are sensitive personal data: the system stores no fingerprint templates and records each employee’s consent.
- Financial, invoice, payroll and personnel records are retained for 10 years (NFR-LEG-03).

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product perspective

The store is a **single physical location** with one website and several social-media channels. The BMS is the single source of truth for products, prices, stock, customers and orders; every channel reads from and writes to it. The system consists of four deliverables sharing one database and one set of business rules:

| Component | Users | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Back office and POS web app** | Owner, manager, cashier, warehouse, online staff, event coordinator, librarian, website editor | Desktop, tablet and phone browser; installable as a PWA; POS works offline. |
| **Website API** | Website front end (built by another party) | Public REST API for catalogue, content, carts, orders and accounts; signed webhooks; preview. |
| **Integration services** | System | GHN, MISA meInvoice, email, fingerprint terminals. |
| **Data platform** | System | Cloud database in Vietnam, backups, audit log, API. |

Social-media channels are not software components: they are sources of orders and traffic. Staff create orders from chats in the back office, and the website API provides shareable product data.

#### External actors and systems

| Actor / system | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Customer / visitor | Browses and orders on the website (a front end that calls this system’s API); pays COD or by bank transfer, scanning the store’s printed QR code and typing the payment reference in the transfer note. |
| Social-media customer | Orders through chat; a staff member enters the order and records the source channel. |
| Website front-end team | Builds the website interface against the API; receives API keys, webhooks, preview tokens and a test environment. |
| GHN | Shipment creation, fee calculation, status updates, COD remittance, address data. |
| MISA meInvoice | Issues, cancels and adjusts e-invoices; emails the invoice to the buyer when requested. |
| Bank transfer | Customer pays by scanning the store’s **printed QR code** (or keying the account number) and types the payment reference in the transfer note; staff confirm receipt by checking the amount and reference. The system does not generate QR codes. |
| Email service | Email only, and only for reminders (events, library), account messages (email verification, password reset) and e-invoice delivery. No SMS or Zalo. |
| Fingerprint terminals | Record employee clock-in/out and send the records to the system. |
| Accountant | Receives tax, sales and payroll exports; does not log in unless granted a role. |

| Role | Main tasks | Typical screens |

| **Manager** (Quản lý) | Day-to-day control: approvals, reports, rosters, promotions, purchasing. | All except owner-only settings. |
| **Cashier** (Thu ngân) | Counter sales, returns, shifts, customer lookup, library desk payments. | POS, Sales, Shift, Customers. |
| **Warehouse keeper** (Thủ kho) | Stock-in, stock counts, catalogue upkeep, handling online orders. | Products, Inventory, Orders & delivery. |
| **Online & social sales staff** | Process website and chat orders, pre-orders, shipments, to-notify worklist. | Orders & delivery, Pre-orders, Customers. |
| **Event coordinator** | Create events, manage registrations, check-in, event merchandise. | Events, Customers. |
| **Librarian** | Library catalogue, members, loans, returns, fines and deposits. | Library. |
| **Website editor** | Maintains website content and structure: pages, news, banners, collections, home sections, menu; previews and publishes. | Website content screens (no prices, stock or settings). |
| **Customer** (website) | Shops and manages their account through the website front end. | Public website (built by another party). |

### 2.4 Operating environment

- Hosted on a server in Vietnam, with backups on S3-compatible storage in Vietnam; staff access through current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS devices.
- In-store: one or more POS stations (PC or tablet), barcode scanner(s), a printer for e-invoice copies, labels and optional sales slips (browser printing), standalone card terminal, fingerprint attendance terminal(s), cash drawer opened by hand, camera barcode scanning on tablets and phones.
- Store internet connection of ordinary broadband quality; the POS tolerates outages (Section 7.3).

### 2.5 Design and implementation constraints

- Staff interface in Vietnamese. Website content (items, categories, pages, posts, banners, collections, menu, SEO text) in **English (required) and Vietnamese (optional)**. Dates are dd/MM/yyyy, time is 24-hour, timezone Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.
- Money is stored and calculated as integer VND; no floating-point arithmetic on amounts.
- Search ignores Vietnamese diacritics and case (“nguyen” finds “Nguyễn”).
- No payment-card data is stored or transmitted; card payments use a standalone terminal.
- Delivery addresses use province and ward (commune) from GHN’s address data; there is no district level.
- Capacity: up to 10 staff users, 20,000 SKUs, fewer than 200 orders per day, events of up to 200 attendees, a library of up to 5,000 titles and 10,000 copies.

### 2.6 Fixed scope decisions

- A library borrower is a registered customer (name and phone). Reading in the room is not tracked; there is no seat or room booking.
- Cart, checkout and customer accounts are provided by this system through the public API.
- Events and the library are managed in the back office from the first release and exposed on the website in release R6.
- GHN is the only carrier. There are no online payment gateways; staff confirm bank transfers manually.
- Orders from social media and marketplaces are entered by staff.
- Statutory social insurance and personal income tax are calculated outside the system; payroll may carry them as manual deduction lines.
- The system has no general-ledger accounting beyond supplier payables and tax reports.

### 3.1 Catalogue (CAT)

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FR-CAT-01 | The system shall maintain an item master for **books**, **stationery**, **other goods** (gifts, toys, bags, etc.) and **bundles**. | M |
| FR-CAT-02 | For books the system shall store ISBN-10/13, title, subtitle, author(s), translator, publisher, publication year, edition, language, page count, cover type, series and cover image. | M |
| FR-CAT-03 | For stationery and other goods the system shall store brand, model, unit of measure and, where relevant, variants (for example colour or size) with their own SKU and barcode. | M |
| FR-CAT-04 | The system shall give every sellable variant a unique SKU, support several barcodes per item, generate internal barcodes for items that have none, and store the **weight in grams** (required, used for delivery fees). | M |
| FR-CAT-05 | The system shall detect duplicates on ISBN, barcode and SKU when an item is created, imported or edited, and block or warn as configured (default: block). | M |
| FR-CAT-06 | The system shall organise items in a configurable category tree of at least three levels, with publisher/brand lists and free tags. | M |
| FR-CAT-07 | The system shall store list price, selling price, cost (visible only with permission), VAT rate per item and price history with effective dates. | M |
| FR-CAT-08 | The system shall record an audit entry for every change to price, cost or VAT rate. | M |
| FR-CAT-09 | The system shall let staff set an item’s status (active, hidden, discontinued) and choose where it is published (POS, website). | M |
| FR-CAT-10 | The system shall store multiple images, a rich-text description and website SEO fields (title, slug, meta description) per item, with Vietnamese and English versions of the translatable text. | M |
| FR-CAT-11 | The system shall import and export items from Excel/CSV with a preview, row-level validation and a downloadable error report, started from the Products screen (“Nhập từ Excel”). SKU and ISBN are separate columns; SKU is required and is the only code shown in item lists. **Images are optional on import:** a spreadsheet cannot carry image files, so an imported item without an image is created and flagged **“missing image”** (“Thiếu ảnh”), shown as a filter on the product list, until staff add a photo on the product page. | M |
| FR-CAT-12 | The system shall support bundles (for example notebook + pen) with their own price; selling a bundle deducts its components from stock. | S |
| FR-CAT-13 | The system shall print shelf/price labels and barcodes in batches using configurable templates. | M |
| FR-CAT-14 | The product detail screen shall show stock by status, movement history, recent sales, supplier(s), and (with permission) cost and margin. | S |
| FR-CAT-15 | The system shall mark titles as **upcoming** (release date) so they can be pre-ordered (FR-ORD-14). | M |

### 3.3 Purchasing, suppliers and consignment (PUR)

Most purchases are small, ad-hoc quantities, so goods are added to stock through a **quick stock-in** screen rather than formal receipt documents.

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FR-PUR-01 | The system shall keep supplier records: name, tax code, contacts, type (publisher, distributor, stationery vendor, individual, consignor), payment terms, bank account, and a flag **“tracks payables”**. | M |
| FR-PUR-02 | **Stock-in of a new product:** one form creates the product and its first stock-in together. Required: SKU, item, product name, category, supplier, cost price, selling price, weight, first quantity, image and description, plus any other field required for a product (FR-CAT). | M |
| FR-PUR-03 | **Stock-in of an existing product:** the form asks only for cost price, selling price and the quantity added. A changed selling price is saved in the price history (FR-CAT-07). | M |
| FR-PUR-04 | Every stock-in receives an automatic internal entry number that identifies it in the stock ledger; no receipt document has to be created. Supplier (for existing products), supplier invoice number, invoice image and note are optional. | M |
| FR-PUR-05 | On stock-in the system shall update moving-average cost and flag items whose new cost makes the selling price unprofitable or below a configured margin. | M |
| FR-PUR-06 | For suppliers flagged “tracks payables” a stock-in may record the amount owed; the system shall keep payments, balances and ageing per supplier, visible only with the payables permission. Other suppliers are treated as paid at stock-in. | M |
| FR-PUR-07 | **Supplier orders (optional):** staff may create an order to a supplier with statuses Draft → Sent → Partially received → Received/Closed, or Cancelled. Receiving goods against an order uses the quick stock-in form. Orders are not required for a stock-in. | S |
| FR-PUR-08 | The system shall roll open pre-orders, special orders and low-stock suggestions up into proposed supplier-order lines per supplier. | S |
| FR-PUR-09 | A stock-in or supplier may be marked **consignment**: stock enters at agreed terms but creates **no payable** until sold. | M |
| FR-PUR-10 | The system shall produce a consignment statement per supplier and period listing quantity received, sold, returned and remaining, the agreed discount, and the amount due. | M |
| FR-PUR-11 | Approving a consignment statement shall create the payable for the sold quantity; payment is then handled as in FR-PUR-06. | M |
| FR-PUR-12 | The system shall record the return of unsold consignment stock with a return entry that reduces stock without cost impact. | M |
| FR-PUR-13 | The system shall alert before agreed consignment settlement or return deadlines. | S |
| FR-PUR-14 | **Bulk stock-in from Excel/CSV:** from the Stock-in screen staff may upload a file (template provided) instead of entering lines one by one. Columns: **SKU** (required; the store's own code), **ISBN** (optional, a separate column; never merged with or used as the SKU), product name, category, supplier, cost price, selling price, weight, quantity, and optional supplier invoice number and note. **The whole file becomes one stock-in entry** with one entry number (FR-PUR-04). A row whose SKU exists adds stock to that product (as FR-PUR-03); a row whose SKU does not exist creates a new product, so it must carry every field FR-PUR-02 requires **except the image**, which is optional on import; such products are flagged “missing image” until staff add a photo (FR-CAT-11). The template therefore also has the columns item type, description and VAT rate. Because the file is one entry, it has **one supplier, one supplier invoice number and one note**: rows may leave these blank, and rows that give different values are errors. Before posting, the system shows a preview with row-level validation, duplicate checks (FR-CAT-05), margin warnings (FR-PUR-05) and a downloadable error report; nothing posts until staff confirm, and the entry posts atomically (all rows or none). | M |

## 5. External Interface Requirements

### 5.1 User interface

- **Shell:** a left navigation grouped into sections (Store, Catalogue & stock, Events, Library, Staff, Website, Reports, Settings), collapsing to a drawer on phones. Menu items and sub-items appear only if the user holds a matching permission; after sign-in the user lands on the first screen they may open (the dashboard for those who can see reports, the POS for a cashier).
- **Lists:** a page title with the primary action (“Add…”) at top right on desktop and under the title on phones; one search box with a filter button that opens a dialog and shows a badge with the number of active filters; multi-select status filters with counts; clearable filters. A row opens the detail page. Tables scroll sideways on phones; long lists page at 40 rows.
- **Status:** colour-coded badges that also carry an icon for states that need attention, so colour is never the only signal.
- **Feedback:** toasts for success and errors; confirmation dialogs for destructive actions; approval dialogs (supervisor PIN) at the point of action.
- **Live screens:** the order inbox refreshes automatically and when the browser tab regains focus; new arrivals are highlighted until opened.
- **Inputs:** money inputs formatted in VND; quantity steppers sized for touch; a hardware barcode scanner and the device camera both work.
- **Printing:** from the browser: e-invoice copies on request, optional sales slips, shelf labels, library slips (optional), payslips and consignment statements. Delivery orders have no printing.
- **Website content screens:** pages and news, collections (with rule builder and live preview of matches), home page sections, menu and footer, banners, and a publish queue with preview, schedule, history and revert.
- **To-notify worklist:** one list of customers to contact, filterable by reason, with a Contacted action.
- **Website:** the website interface is built and owned by a separate front-end team and is outside this system; the system supplies all its data through the public API.

### 6.2 Data quality, retention and migration

- Posted financial documents (sales, stock-ins, invoices, payroll) are never deleted; they are reversed by a new document.
- Financial documents, invoices, payroll and personnel records are kept for 10 years. Customer data is not deleted automatically; personal data is anonymised on request (FR-CRM-08).
- Opening data is loaded through the import wizard (FR-ADM-07): products (price, images, description, category) with opening stock, and customers with their points balance, from the previous system’s Excel exports. Opening supplier balances are entered by hand. Order history of the previous system is not imported.
- All timestamps are stored in UTC and displayed in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.

## 8. Business Rules and Default Parameters

Rules are configurable in Settings; the values below are the defaults.

| ID | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| BR-01 | All amounts are whole VND. Percentage discounts and points are rounded half-up to the nearest dong. |
| BR-02 | Displayed selling prices include VAT; VAT is itemised on invoices. |
| BR-03 | Available stock = on hand − reserved. Reservations expire as defined for each flow. |
| BR-04 | Return window: 7 days, with the sale found in the system by number, phone, date or item; exceptions need approval. Damaged returns do not restock. |
| BR-05 | Cashier discount limit: up to 10% per line. Above the limit needs a supervisor. There is no category discount cap unless one is set. |
| BR-06 | Shift close: any non-zero variance needs a reason note; closing is never blocked; variances above 50,000 VND send managers an in-app notification. |
| BR-07 | Loyalty: 1 point per 10,000 VND net spend; 1 point redeems 100 VND; minimum 50 points to redeem; points expire after 12 months. Event tickets, deposits and fines earn no points. Redeemed points are a discount on the sale, not a payment method. |
| BR-08 | Tiers (rolling 12 months): Standard; Silver from 3,000,000 VND; Gold from 10,000,000 VND. |
| BR-09 | Unpaid bank-transfer orders auto-cancel after 24 hours. Prepaid event holds expire after 60 minutes. No-prepayment tickets do not expire. |
| BR-10 | Pre-order deposit: 30% or full payment; uncollected pre-orders and special orders are held 7 days after the customer is contacted. |
| BR-11 | Consignment: payable arises only on sale; statements monthly at the agreed supplier discount. |
| BR-12 | Events: full refund of a paid ticket if the store cancels the event or the registrant cancels at least 48 hours before. A waiting-list offer gives 12 hours to pay a prepaid ticket. |
| BR-13 | Library: loan period 14 days; up to 2 renewals of 7 days; up to 3 loans at once; deposit 200,000 VND per member; fine 2,000 VND per copy per day with no grace and a cap equal to the copy value; lost copy = copy value + 20,000 VND processing fee. |
| BR-14 | A deposit is a liability to the member, not revenue; fines and charges are revenue when raised. |
| BR-15 | Members with overdue loans or unpaid fines cannot borrow or renew. |
| BR-16 | Customer phone number is unique; new orders from a known phone link to the existing profile. |
| BR-17 | Posted documents are never deleted. Corrections are made by reversal, with reason and approval. |
| BR-18 | Overtime multipliers: 150% on a weekday, 200% on a weekly rest day, 300% on a public holiday. |
| BR-19 | Points are credited when payment completes and are reversed proportionally on a return. |
| BR-20 | Visitors only ever see items published to the website; unpublished or empty targets (menu items, banners, sections) are hidden automatically. |
| BR-21 | Scheduled website changes go live at the stated time in Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh; the previous published version is always kept for revert. |
| BR-22 | Stock display on the website defaults to the exact available quantity (on hand − reserved). Staff may switch the whole site or a single product to few left (below 5), in stock / out of stock, or hidden. |
| BR-23 | Staff editing website content cannot change prices, stock or settings; publishing needs a separate permission. |
| BR-24 | E-invoices: every completed sale is invoiced (`every_sale`, default) and the invoice is emailed only when the customer asks; offline sales are invoiced after reconnection. |
| BR-25 | Customer email is limited to event and library reminders and account messages. All other customer contact (pre-order arrival, waitlist offers, event cancellation, library holds, pickup ready) is by staff phone/Zalo from the to-notify worklist. |
| BR-26 | Payroll: hourly pay plus sales bonus, monthly period; statutory insurance and personal income tax are outside the system. |
| BR-27 | Fingerprint templates are never stored in the system; employee consent is recorded. |
| BR-28 | Delivery orders are not printed; the tracking number is shown on screen. |
