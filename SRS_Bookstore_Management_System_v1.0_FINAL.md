## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional requirements of the **Bookstore Management System** (BMS): one integrated platform that runs the day-to-day operations of a single bookstore that sells books, stationery and other goods, hosts events, and operates a lending library space. It is the basis for design, construction and acceptance testing.

### 1.2 Scope

The BMS covers every operating activity of the store and its sales channels:

- **In-store retail** – point of sale, shifts and cash handling, returns, e-invoices.
- **Catalogue, inventory and purchasing** – books, stationery and other goods; quick stock-in; stock counts; suppliers; consignment stock.
- **Website back end (public API)** – the system holds and controls everything the website shows and does: products, prices, stock, collections, content pages, news, menu, carts, orders and customer accounts, exposed through a documented public API. Staff control **what appears** (content, collections, home-page sections, menu, stock display). The **website interface itself is built by a separate party and is not part of this system.**
- **Social media channels** – orders arriving through Facebook, Zalo, Instagram, TikTok and similar are entered by staff, tagged with their source, and reported by channel.
- **Online orders and delivery** – order inbox, fulfilment, GHN integration, cash on delivery, pre-orders.
- **Events** – free and paid events, registrations, check-in, merchandise, recurring programmes and event results.
- **Library** – separate lending collection with its own library codes, paid loans, late fees and lost-book charges.
- **Customers and loyalty** – customer profiles, points and member tiers, promotions.
- **Staff** – employee files, shift scheduling, fingerprint timekeeping and hourly payroll calculation.
- **Reporting and administration** – dashboards, sales, inventory, tax and audit reports; roles and permissions; settings; backup.

**Out of scope:** online payment gateways (VNPay, MoMo, ZaloPay); the website front end (interface design and build); automatic order import from marketplaces; carriers other than GHN; paid membership programmes, seat or room rental, used-book buy-back and gift cards; marketing-campaign tooling; SMS and Zalo messages; customer emails other than reminders, account messages and e-invoice delivery; returns to suppliers other than consignment returns; general-ledger accounting beyond supplier payables and tax reports; statutory payroll (social insurance and personal income tax). Website exposure of events and the library is delivered in release R6 (Section 9).

### 1.4 Regulatory basis

- E-invoices follow the Vietnamese e-invoice regulation (Decree 123/2020/ND-CP and its amendments). The store is a company declaring VAT by the deduction method.
- Personal data handling follows Decree 13/2023/ND-CP. Fingerprints are sensitive personal data: the system stores no fingerprint templates and records each employee’s consent.
- Financial, invoice, payroll and personnel records are retained for 10 years (NFR-LEG-03).

## 2. Overall Description

### 2.1 Product perspective

The store is a **single physical location** with one website and several social-media channels. The BMS is the single source of truth for products, prices, stock, customers and orders; every channel reads from and writes to it. The system consists of four deliverables sharing one database and one set of business rules:

| Component | Users | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Back office and POS web app** | Owner, manager, cashier, warehouse, online staff, event coordinator, librarian, website editor | Desktop, tablet and phone browser; installable as a PWA; POS works offline. |
| **Website API** | Website front end (built by another party) | Public REST API for catalogue, content, carts, orders and accounts; signed webhooks; preview. |
| **Integration services** | System | GHN, MISA meInvoice, email, fingerprint terminals. |
| **Data platform** | System | Cloud database in Vietnam, backups, audit log, API. |

Social-media channels are not software components: they are sources of orders and traffic. Staff create orders from chats in the back office, and the website API provides shareable product data.

#### External actors and systems

| Actor / system | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Customer / visitor | Browses and orders on the website (a front end that calls this system’s API); pays COD or by bank transfer, scanning the store’s printed QR code and typing the payment reference in the transfer note. |
| Social-media customer | Orders through chat; a staff member enters the order and records the source channel. |
| Website front-end team | Builds the website interface against the API; receives API keys, webhooks, preview tokens and a test environment. |
| GHN | Shipment creation, fee calculation, status updates, COD remittance, address data. |
| MISA meInvoice | Issues, cancels and adjusts e-invoices; emails the invoice to the buyer when requested. |
| Bank transfer | Customer pays by scanning the store’s **printed QR code** (or keying the account number) and types the payment reference in the transfer note; staff confirm receipt by checking the amount and reference. The system does not generate QR codes. |
| Email service | Email only, and only for event reminders, account messages (email verification, password reset) and e-invoice delivery. No SMS or Zalo. |
| Fingerprint terminals | Record employee clock-in/out and send the records to the system. |
| Accountant | Receives tax, sales and payroll exports; does not log in unless granted a role. |

Roles are data, not code: the owner can edit permissions per role (FR-ADM-03). The defaults are:

| **Cashier** (Thu ngân) | Counter sales, returns, pre-orders, shifts, customer lookup. | POS, Sales, Shift, Customers. |
| **Warehouse keeper** (Thủ kho) | Stock-in, stock counts, catalogue upkeep, handling online orders. | Products, Inventory, Orders & delivery. |
| **Online & social sales staff** | Process website and chat orders and shipments. | Orders & delivery, Customers. |

| **Librarian** | Library catalogue and labels, loans, returns, renewals, reservations and charges. | Library. |

| **Customer** (website) | Shops and manages their account through the website front end. | Public website (built by another party). |

### 2.4 Operating environment

- Hosted on a server in Vietnam, with backups on S3-compatible storage in Vietnam; staff access through current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on Windows, macOS, Android and iOS devices.
- In-store: one or more POS stations (PC or tablet), barcode scanner(s), a printer for e-invoice copies, labels and optional sales slips (browser printing), standalone card terminal, fingerprint attendance terminal(s), cash drawer opened by hand, camera barcode scanning on tablets and phones.
- Store internet connection of ordinary broadband quality; the POS tolerates outages (Section 7.3).

### 2.5 Design and implementation constraints

- Staff interface in Vietnamese. Website content (items, categories, pages, posts, banners, collections, menu, SEO text) in **English (required) and Vietnamese (optional)**. Dates are dd/MM/yyyy, time is 24-hour, timezone Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh.
- Money is stored and calculated as integer VND; no floating-point arithmetic on amounts.
- Search ignores Vietnamese diacritics and case (“nguyen” finds “Nguyễn”).
- No payment-card data is stored or transmitted; card payments use a standalone terminal.
- Delivery addresses use province and ward (commune) from GHN’s address data; there is no district level.
- Capacity: up to 10 staff users, 20,000 SKUs, fewer than 200 orders per day, events of up to 200 attendees, a library of up to 5,000 titles and 10,000 copies.

### 2.6 Fixed scope decisions

- A library borrower is a registered customer (name and phone), found by phone; there is no library card or membership record. Borrowers pay a loan fee per copy and no deposit (BR-13); photos taken at the desk stay outside the system. Reading in the room is not tracked; there is no seat or room booking.
- Cart, checkout and customer accounts are provided by this system through the public API.
- Events and the library are managed in the back office from the first release and exposed on the website in release R6.
- GHN is the only carrier. There are no online payment gateways; staff confirm bank transfers manually.
- Orders from social media and marketplaces are entered by staff.
- Statutory social insurance and personal income tax are calculated outside the system; payroll may carry them as manual deduction lines.
- The system has no general-ledger accounting and no supplier payables; only tax reports.

### 3.9 Library (LIB)

The library is a **separate lending collection** with its own catalogue; its books are not sale stock. Borrowers are registered customers who pay a loan fee for each copy they borrow and are subject to late fees and lost-book charges. No deposit is taken and there is no library card (deposits may be enabled later by policy, FR-LIB-09). Loan parameters are in Section 8.

#### 3.9.1 Collection

The **library code** is set by staff for each title, is unique, and is suggested with a language prefix (`VN-`, `EN-`) that staff may change. ## 5. External Interface Requirements

### 5.1 User interface

- **Shell:** a left navigation grouped into sections (Store, Catalogue & stock, Events, Library, Staff, Website, Reports, Settings), collapsing to a drawer on phones. Menu items and sub-items appear only if the user holds a matching permission; after sign-in the user lands on the first screen they may open (the dashboard for those who can see reports, the POS for a cashier).
- **Lists:** a page title with the primary action (“Add…”) at top right on desktop and under the title on phones; one search box with a filter button that opens a dialog and shows a badge with the number of active filters; multi-select status filters with counts; clearable filters. A row opens the detail page. Tables scroll sideways on phones; long lists page at 40 rows.
- **Status:** colour-coded badges that also carry an icon for states that need attention, so colour is never the only signal.
- **Feedback:** toasts for success and errors; confirmation dialogs for destructive actions; approval dialogs (supervisor PIN) at the point of action.
- **Live screens:** the order inbox refreshes automatically and when the browser tab regains focus; new arrivals are highlighted until opened.
- **Inputs:** money inputs formatted in VND; quantity steppers sized for touch; a hardware barcode scanner and the device camera both work.
- **Printing:** from the browser: e-invoice copies on request, optional sales slips, shelf labels, library slips (optional), payslips and consignment statements. Delivery orders have no printing.
- **Website content screens:** pages and news, collections (with rule builder and live preview of matches), home page sections, menu and footer, banners, and a publish queue with preview, schedule, history and revert.
- **Website:** the website interface is built and owned by a separate front-end team and is outside this system; the system supplies all its data through the public API.

### 6.2 Data quality, retention and migration

- Posted financial documents (sales, stock-ins, invoices, payroll) are never deleted; they are reversed by a new document.

### B.4 Library copy and loan

| Copy status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Available (Còn) | On the shelf, lendable. |
| Reserved (Đã giữ) | Held for the borrower first in the reservation queue. |
| On loan (Đang mượn) | Borrowed; due date set. |
| Lost (Mất) | Charge raised; out of circulation; back to Available if found (FR-LIB-11). |
| Damaged (Hỏng) | Charge raised or returned damaged; out of circulation. |

A **loan** is Open until the copy is returned and **Overdue** while open past its due date (late fee accruing, borrower blocked, BR-15); Overdue is a loan state, not a copy status.
